CLIMATE ACTION

Small changes can help

Aither focuses on practical actions instead of complicated lifestyle changes.

01

Travel smarter

Combine trips where possible and consider public transport for suitable journeys.

02

Use energy carefully

Switch off unused appliances and pay attention to unnecessary electricity consumption.

03

Choose sustainable food

Increasing plant-based food choices can be one way to reduce the impact of everyday meals.

04

Buy thoughtfully

Avoid unnecessary purchases and look for durable products that can be used for longer.

ABOUT AITHER AI

Making climate action more understandable.

Aither AI is a student project created to show how artificial intelligence and simple digital tools can help people understand everyday environmental choices.

The idea is not to replace scientific carbon accounting. Instead, it works as a simple awareness and decision-support prototype.

Responsible AI

Fairness

Recommendations should consider different lifestyles and circumstances.

Transparency

Estimates are clearly presented as approximate prototype calculations.

Privacy

The prototype does not require personal identifying information.

Ethics

Suggestions are designed to inform users without blaming or shaming them.
